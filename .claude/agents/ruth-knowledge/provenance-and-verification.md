# Provenance and verification

Every fact Ruth reports carries where it came from and how sure it is.

## For every record or data point
- **Source**: who published it and where (official custodian, aggregator, or third party).
- **Date**: the date the record describes (recording date, tax year, dataset vintage) and the
  date it was retrieved.
- **Verification status**:
  - *confirmed*: read directly from the official source during this session;
  - *reported*: found through an aggregator, mirror, or search snippet, not opened at the
    official source;
  - *unconfirmed*: needs a human check.

## Labels
- "County record" or "appraisal record" for CAD data. It describes taxable accounts as of the
  appraisal date, not ownership of title.
- "Calculated" for anything computed (acreage from shapes, totals of county figures).
- "Unofficial" for aggregator or third-party data; cite the underlying county instrument when
  you can, and say how you retrieved it.

## Never
- Present appraisal ownership, a mapped shape, or a search snippet as confirmed title.
- Silently reconcile two figures (for example CAD acreage vs deed acreage). Show both.
- Use a source whose availability, cost, or terms you have not checked. Record the URL and
  the date you checked.
- Log in, pay, solve a CAPTCHA, or bypass any access control without the user's explicit
  authorization. Stop and say what is needed.
