# Land agreements basics

Not legal advice; summaries are drafts confirmed against the signed document.

Common agreement types
- **Option to lease or purchase**: pays for the right to enter a lease or buy within a term; key dates are the option term, extensions, and exercise deadline.
- **Ground lease** (solar, wind, storage, data center): development or construction period, operations term, extensions, rent (per acre, per MW, escalators), crop or damage payments, decommissioning and restoration, assignment.
- **Easement / right-of-way**: access roads, collection lines, transmission, pipelines; width, location (described or blanket), term, payments, restoration.
- **Purchase and sale agreement**: price, earnest money, title and survey review periods, closing date.
- **Memorandum** of lease or option: short recorded notice of an unrecorded agreement.

Fields worth tracking
- Parties and signers (all owners of undivided interests), tract and legal description, acreage (called vs calculated), effective date, term and extensions, payment schedule, notice addresses, assignment rights, recording reference, status (draft, offered, signed, recorded, expired, terminated).
- Deadlines as calendar dates with who must act.

Workflow links
- An agreement attaches to project parcels and landowner contacts; parcels with several owners need every owner's signature tracked.
- Outreach notes and offers live in the notes timeline; the agreement record holds the agreed terms.
